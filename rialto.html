Factory-built construction scales beyond the single-family home.

Rialto Apartments is a 16-unit multifamily fire rebuild demonstrating how the 4C SAFE™ system can be applied to larger residential projects where repetition, coordination, and construction efficiency become even more valuable.

Factory-BuiltPre-engineered components

Rather than framing each wall entirely in the field, 4C brings construction into a controlled manufacturing environment. Building components are engineered, manufactured, and organized as a coordinated system before arriving on site—helping simplify field installation and reduce dependence on conventional site-built framing.

Repeatable by design

Reduced onsite labor

The System

Cold-formed steel, built for fire resistance.

4C SAFE™ combines cold-formed steel framing with tested fire-resistive assemblies and a factory-controlled manufacturing process. The system is designed to integrate into conventional construction while shifting a significant portion of framing work off site.
