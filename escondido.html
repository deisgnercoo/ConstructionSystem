4C SAFE® · Single-Family Fire Rebuild

A fire rebuild, built differently.

The Escondido Residence is a 1,894 sq ft single-family fire rebuild and the first complete field installation of the 4C SAFE® system. The project demonstrates how factory-built construction can accelerate the structural phase of a custom home while working within a conventional construction process.

1,894 SFSingle-family residence
3 DaysExterior wall package installed
0Window & door opening adjustments

Wall components arrived pre-engineered, pre-labeled, and ready for assembly. By the end of Day 2, approximately 75% of the exterior walls were installed. By Day 3, the exterior wall package was complete, followed by roof framing, interior partitions, and waterproofing.

The Build

From factory panels to a sealed structure.

The project also demonstrated the precision of factory-built construction: windows and doors were installed without field modifications, with openings manufactured to tight tolerances before the components ever arrived on site.

Pre-engineered wall panels

Exterior wall components arrived pre-labeled and ready for assembly, with no on-site fabrication required before they went up.

Zero field adjustments

Window and door openings were manufactured to tight tolerances before reaching the site, so every opening fit without modification.

3-day wall package

Exterior walls reached roughly 75% complete by the end of Day 2 and finished on Day 3, followed by roof framing and interior partitions.
