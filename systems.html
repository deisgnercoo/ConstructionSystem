Our Process

Any design. One end-to-end process.

From design to factory-built components, delivered and installed. 4C works with the full project team to convert the design into a coordinated building system. One 4C scope covers exterior wall panels, interior partitions, and roof and floor framing through installation and warranty.

The Six Phases

One coordinated path from design to warranty.

4C brings design coordination, manufacturing, and field execution into one streamlined process, from your first building design through a fully warrantied, installed system.

01

Design

Any design, modeled in Revit and backed by certified 4C details.

02

Coordination

We coordinate with the full project team, city, engineers, and suppliers.

03

Manufacturing

Precision factory production with modern MRP, less waste, and predictable output.

04

Delivery

Packaged, protected, and sequenced for on-time installation.

05

Installation

Installed by our licensed team and coordinated with factory production and delivery.

06

Warranty

Industry-leading warranty and support for the complete installed system.

What We Manufacture

Two component systems, one manufacturing standard.

Depending on your project's configuration, the Manufacturing phase produces components under one of two systems. Both are precision-engineered, factory-built, ICC-ES listed, and patent pending.

Shared Platform

What every 4C component has in common.

Regardless of which system your configuration calls for, every 4C component is manufactured to the same standard, reflecting the four pillars described on our About page.

Integrated exterior components

Framing, insulation, sheathing, and a weather-resistant barrier arrive as a single, integrated component.

Prefabricated interior walls

Interior wall components are manufactured off-site and delivered ready to set in place.

Prefab roof & floor framing

Roof and floor framing components are pre-engineered and pre-cut, cutting hours of onsite layout work.

Every 4C component is precision-manufactured, pre-labeled, and delivered installation-ready. The coordinated system installs quickly, reducing costs, onsite labor, complexity, and schedule risk.
