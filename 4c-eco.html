Overview

Engineered for cost efficiency and comfort.

4C ECO™ is our wood-framed panel system, purpose-built for Type V construction and optimized for balanced cost and thermal performance. It carries the highest thermal efficiency of our two systems, and is well suited to multifamily and infill projects, like our Escondido project, where budget control matters as much as speed. For higher fire-risk sites, our steel-framed 4C SAFE® system may be the better fit; see the Systems overview for a side-by-side comparison, or read more about the sustainability case for both.
